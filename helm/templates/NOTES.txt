Internal mTLS: the chart made its own CA in {{ include "yarilo.fullname" . }}-internal-ca and
keeps it across upgrades by reading the cluster. A renderer that cannot read the
cluster (helm template, Argo CD and similar) makes a new CA on every render and
breaks internal links between pods from different syncs: set
internalTLS.ca.secretName to a CA Secret made once, or internalTLS.certManager.
